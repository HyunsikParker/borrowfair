Transcript

  1. BorrowFair plans equipment loans. This fictional club has 12 requests from 11 borrowers.
  2. The same stock serves 9 borrowers here. The first-come comparison serves 6. These are example results.
  3. The rule serves more borrowers first, then prefers a lower total of previous loans. Each borrower gets at most one request.
  4. Requests can need several items together. Dara's camera-and-microphone kit conflicts on the listed dates.
  5. Keep a specific request when it must be included. Keeping Nora's long loan reduces this plan to 6 borrowers.
  6. Release the keep to compare the full set again. A draft allocation is not a confirmed reservation.
  7. One extra tripod lets Ian join the plan. The comparison reports additions and displacement without changing stock.
  8. Apply the extra unit only if it is actually available. This workspace now plans for 3 tripods and 10 borrowers.
  9. Edit dates, quantities and prior-loan counts. Return dates are free for another pickup; aliases keep contact details out.
  10. Export a workspace backup. Restoring a file requires a preview and confirmation before it replaces current work.
  11. The limits are visible: one local round, at most 48 requests, and no checkout or synchronization service.
  12. Built for GenHacks with OpenAI Codex assistance. Code, tests and documentation accompany the working prototype.

The recording demonstrates a tested prototype, not a field deployment or a competition result.
