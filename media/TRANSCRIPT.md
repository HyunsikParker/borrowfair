# BorrowFair walkthrough

Silent screen recording of actual browser interactions. Timed English captions were composited into the reserved strip below the app after capture; the app area was not altered.

0.2–8.2 s: BorrowFair plans equipment loans. This fictional club has 12 requests from 11 borrowers.

8.2–18.2 s: The same stock serves 9 borrowers here. The first-come comparison serves 6. These are example results.

18.3–29.3 s: The rule serves more borrowers first, then prefers a lower total of previous loans. Each borrower gets at most one request.

29.4–40.4 s: Requests can need several items together. Dara’s camera-and-microphone kit conflicts on the listed dates.

40.5–50.5 s: Keep a specific request when it must be included. Keeping Nora’s long loan reduces this plan to 6 borrowers.

50.6–58.6 s: Release the keep to compare the full set again. A draft allocation is not a confirmed reservation.

58.7–70.7 s: One extra tripod lets Ian join the plan. The comparison reports additions and displacement without changing stock.

70.8–79.8 s: Apply the extra unit only if it is actually available. This workspace now plans for 3 tripods and 10 borrowers.

79.9–89.9 s: Edit dates, quantities and prior-loan counts. Return dates are free for another pickup; aliases keep contact details out.

90.1–98.1 s: Export a workspace backup. Restoring a file requires a preview and confirmation before it replaces current work.

98.2–108.2 s: The limits are visible: one local round, at most 48 requests, and no checkout or synchronization service.

108.2–116.2 s: Built for GenHacks with OpenAI Codex assistance. Code, tests and documentation accompany the working prototype.
